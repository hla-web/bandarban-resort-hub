# Image sources

## Reference-inspired redesign artwork

`clientend/public/images/hillside-hero-v2.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the customer-site redesign. It is an illustrative fictional hillside lodge, used only for the hero and trip banner. Property and room cards continue to use the images stored in the backend by hotel management. The original generated image was copied into the project without overwriting an existing asset.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide panoramic hero background for Bandarban Resort Hub, a responsive resort discovery website. Generate a single high-quality photorealistic landscape photograph, approximately 3:1 panoramic composition. A warm wooden hillside resort lodge occupies the RIGHT THIRD of the image, with broad covered veranda, dark sloping roof, simple wooden chairs and soft amber interior lights. Dense tropical green forest covers rolling hills of Bandarban Bangladesh, layers of blue-green ridges and white valley mist spreading across the LEFT TWO THIRDS into the distance. Natural leafy branches frame top corners. Late afternoon with warm pale peach light near horizon, richly natural green foliage, quiet inviting atmosphere. Leave uncluttered dark forest and distant hills on left for large white website text; no text should appear in the generated asset. Camera from a hillside slightly above neighboring valley, eye-level lodge balcony on right. Tropical Bangladeshi hills, no alpine snow, no tall mountains, no pool, no people, no logos, no lettering, no watermark. It is an illustrative fictional resort photograph, not an image of a specific real hotel. Detailed believable timber, tasteful architecture, editorial travel photography rather than a 3D render.
